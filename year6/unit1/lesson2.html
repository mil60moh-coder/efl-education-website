Unit 1 – Lesson 2

My free time activities

This lesson focuses on describing free time activities and talking about how often learners do them.

Official syllabus – Lesson objectives

  • Talk about free time activities using simple sentences
  • Describe how often activities are done
  • Use basic time expressions correctly

Core vocabulary

  • drawing
  • shopping
  • gardening

Grammar points

  • Adverbs of frequency: always, usually, sometimes, never
  • Prepositions of time: on Monday, in the morning

21st-century skills and values

  • Self-expression
  • Time management
  • Personal responsibility

Core Principle

Work smarter, not harder.
Organise your time and practise regularly instead of rushing at the last minute.
