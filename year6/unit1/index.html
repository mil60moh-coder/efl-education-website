Unit 1 – Entertaining myself

This unit focuses on free time activities, games, and leisure. Learners develop the ability to talk about what they like doing, how they spend their free time, and how they interact with others.

Unit objectives

  • Talk about free time activities and games
  • Express likes, preferences, and simple reasons
  • Participate in simple social interactions

Core language

  • Free time activities vocabulary
  • Like / enjoy + verb + -ing
  • Suggestions and simple invitations

Values and life skills

  • Responsible use of free time
  • Cooperation and fair play
  • Making choices and respecting others

Core Principle

Work smarter, not harder.
Think carefully, plan, and make intelligent choices rather than just repeating work.
